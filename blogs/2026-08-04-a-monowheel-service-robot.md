A monowheel service robot combines the speed, range, and compact footprint of a high-performance electric unicycle with a practical robotic upper body equipped with cameras, sensors, storage, and dexterous arms. Designed around a road-going electric-unicycle drivetrain, it would be capable of highway speeds and hundreds of kilometres of range without sacrificing the precision needed to navigate indoors at walking pace. It could open doors, use elevators, inspect equipment, deliver supplies, and provide rapid-response telepresence for emergency or industrial teams. By treating wheel torque, body lean, arm movement, cargo weight, and environmental contact as one whole-body control system, the robot could move naturally across roads and human infrastructure while remaining capable of meaningful interaction once it arrives.
